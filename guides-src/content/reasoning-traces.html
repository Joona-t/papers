An encrypted reasoning block can still leak if another model can be persuaded to reveal its contents. This brief guide summarises the authors' abstract, not the full experimental results.

Hidden does not mean safe to share

The authors report that encrypted reasoning blocks can be reused across sessions, users and models within a provider. A weaker model can expose a stronger model's hidden reasoning.

They describe risks to proprietary reasoning, private data and safety, plus prompt injections concealed inside encrypted blocks.

Check yourself

Does a safe final answer prove the hidden reasoning is safe to disclose? No: the authors report hazardous information in reasoning even when the visible answer refuses.

Composition is the security boundary

The reported weakness is interchangeability of client-held encrypted traces, rather than a demonstrated mathematical break of encryption.

The abstract describes tests across Anthropic, OpenAI and Google, responsible disclosure, and proposed cryptographic and system mitigations. Consult the paper for model versions, attack conditions and mitigation details; this summary does not establish current provider vulnerability.
